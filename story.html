The story behind IntrosHIrts

It all started with a simple “HI.”

One snowstorm. Six classmates. A deck of cards in the library.

My why

I grew up as an introvert; the shy, quiet one in the corner. As I entered my teenage years (where it became harder to make friends), I started relying on my hair and my outfits as conversation starters.

I realized that once someone broke the ice, I would get comfortable and be less introverted (never extroverted, though).

I always had the mindset that no one liked me or thought I was weird, so I refrained from reaching out and starting a conversation.

In grade 9, I had an eye opening experience: the people in my French class were actually really fun and friendly. I came to this conclusion near the end of the semester during a snowstorm where the 6 of us who showed up to school that day played card games in the library.

When I got home, I realized that I could’ve had those friendships earlier in the year if I had just said a simple “HI.”

I hope to use this brand to help bring us introverts together…

everyone is quietly searching for kindred spirits!

— Rekha Ramdass, Founder, IntrosHIrts ♡
